Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust has transformed how women engage with maternity services by creating a single digital front door through its Patient Engagement Portal, integrated with the NHS App. Powered by Patients Know Best, this personal health record platform combines administrative functionality with advanced interactive tools which enable clinical engagement and educational support.
