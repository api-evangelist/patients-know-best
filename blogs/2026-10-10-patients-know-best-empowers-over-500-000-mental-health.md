On World Mental Health Day, Patients Know Best (PKB) has reached another milestone in digital healthcare: over 500,000 registered service users across England now have direct access to their health records through the PKB platform. PKB currently supports more than a third of Mental Health Trusts in England. Alongside this, all patients in England can request to add their GP data, providing them with a single patient record.
