Every year, hundreds of people are diagnosed with Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia (CLL), a type of blood cancer, in the Netherlands. While many treatments exist, the medical guidelines are incredibly complex, making it difficult for patients to understand which option best fits their unique life and genetic profile. This case study illustrates how CMyLife used the Patients Know Best (PKB) platform and its flexible APIs to launch a digital "Decision Aid".
