In early 2025, University Hospitals Sussex (UHSussex) became the first trust in the region to switch off paper appointment letters for all patients registered with their Personal Health Record - Patients Know Best (PKB). This was driven by the need to reduce duplication, cut costs, and modernise patient communication.
