East Sussex Healthcare Trust (ESHT) recorded 756 A&E attendances for migraine over a 12-month period. Standard A&E environment - bright, loud and busy, worsens symptoms like photo/phonophobia, so patients in crisis were feeling much worse as well as getting the wrong level of care.
